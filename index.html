The Founder

A founder-led company,
built to grow.

Bett KE Tech started with a simple goal: build useful software for real problems in Kenya. Today, the company is school-first, with products designed around the daily work of teachers, school administrators, and businesses.

The company is intentionally built to stay close to its customers. Whether it is a school system or a business platform, you communicate directly with the person leading the build — from the first idea to launch and support.

Bett's approach starts with understanding the problem before writing a line of code, focuses on functionality over technology for its own sake, and treats every product as something that keeps improving as users' needs change.
