About Bett KE Tech

Built in Kenya, for the problems Kenyan schools and businesses actually have.

We're a school-first software company that also builds custom digital systems for businesses — practical, affordable, and supported by the same person who built it.

Our Story

How we got here.

Bett KE Tech began with a simple observation: Kenyan schools and small businesses were stuck choosing between expensive foreign software that didn't fit local workflows, or no software at all. We set out to close that gap.

What started as a single school marks system has grown into a school-first software company, with a widening set of tools for finance, timetabling, and business operations across Kenya.

2025
Bett KE Tech founded
Started building school and business software with a focus on local needs — M-Pesa, CBC, and Kenyan admin realities baked in from day one.
2025
CBE Mark Sheet System launches
Our flagship product goes live — a full CBC-aligned marks and reporting platform for Grades 1–12, with M-Pesa billing built in.
2026
Finance & timetable packages
Two ready-to-deploy school packages launch, extending the product line beyond marks into fee tracking, auditing, and clash-free scheduling.
Now
Growing school-first, staying founder-led
Every product is still designed and built directly by the founder — one point of contact, from first idea to long-term support.
Our Mission

To make reliable, affordable software the default for Kenyan schools and businesses — not the exception.

The Founder

A founder-led company, built to grow.

Bett KE Tech started with a simple goal: build useful software for real problems in Kenya. Today, the company is school-first, with products designed around the daily work of teachers, school administrators, and businesses.

The company is intentionally built to stay close to its customers. Whether it is a school system or a business platform, you communicate directly with the person leading the build — from the first idea to launch and support.

Bett's approach starts with understanding the problem before writing a line of code, focuses on functionality over technology for its own sake, and treats every product as something that keeps improving as users' needs change.
